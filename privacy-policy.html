Mimi Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026
Publisher / data controller: Kundendu Singh
Privacy contact: kundendu@icloud.com

Mimi is a children’s app for short, grown-up-supervised, real-world play. This policy describes Mimi for iOS version 1.0.0.

Information stored on your device

Mimi saves parent-selected age range, language, narration and session-length preferences, whether setup is complete, and up to 20 finished session summaries. A summary records the date, session duration, activity identifiers marked done or skipped, and how the session ended. These details are stored in Mimi’s local app data on the device. Mimi does not operate an account or server that receives this information. Apple device backup or migration may include app data according to the device owner’s Apple settings and Apple’s services.

Mimi does not ask for a child’s name, birth date, voice recording, photo, contact details, or location. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. It does not run a publisher-operated server. If a grown-up opens Mimi Plus, Apple’s StoreKit may contact the App Store to show subscription details, process or restore a purchase, and provide a signed subscription entitlement. Apple processes payment information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy; Mimi does not receive payment credentials. Mimi uses the entitlement result on-device to unlock paid activities and does not send it to the publisher.

Voice and activities

When narration is enabled or Listen is tapped, Mimi plays pre-generated English and Hindi recordings of fixed prompts written for this app. These recordings are bundled with Mimi and play offline; no child data is sent to a voice service. If a recording is unavailable, the device may use on-device speech synthesis. Mimi does not record or listen to the child, provide live AI conversation, or generate new activity instructions.

Parent controls and deletion

An adult should set up and supervise the child’s use. The grown-up addition question is only an interface gate; it is not identity verification or verified parental consent. An adult can review Mimi’s session recaps and use Grown-ups → Settings → Delete all local data to clear saved settings and summaries. Deleting the app also removes its local data, subject to any device backups.

Children’s privacy

Mimi is designed for children to use with a grown-up nearby. The app does not collect or transmit children’s personal information to the publisher. Parents or guardians can contact the publisher with privacy questions or requests.

Changes and contact

If Mimi’s data practices change, the publisher will update this policy and its effective date. For privacy questions, contact kundendu@icloud.com.
